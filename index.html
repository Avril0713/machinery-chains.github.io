About Us

Professional manufacturer committed to quality and innovation

Ningbo Discovery Machinery Parts Factory

Our company mainly produces various conveying chains assembled from chain links manufactured through Lost Wax precision casting process. We can select the appropriate materials according to the customer's requirements, including carbon steel, low alloy steel, stainless steel, high manganese steel, etc., applying suitable heat treatment as per needs.
